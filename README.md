
### My favorite movie
**Cosmic Princess Kaguya**

---

Reimagining The Tale of the Bamboo Cutter in a contemporary setting, the story follows 17-year-old Iroha Sakayori, who balances school and part-time work. Her life changes when Kaguya, a spirited runaway from the Moon, enters her world and draws her into performing inside Tsukuyomi, a virtual metaverse filled with music, virtual idols, and action.
